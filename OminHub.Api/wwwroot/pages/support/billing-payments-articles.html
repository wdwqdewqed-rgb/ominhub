Payout Schedule & Methods

Understand when and how you receive payments, including bank transfers, PayPal, wire transfers, and payment thresholds.

Payout Schedule:
  • Processing Date: 15th of each month (or next business day)
  • Minimum Threshold: $100 USD (or local equivalent)
  • Processing Time: 3-5 business days after initiation
  • Cut-off Date: Last day of previous month for earnings
Available Methods:
  • Bank Transfer (ACH): Free, 3-5 business days
  • PayPal: Instant, 2.9% + $0.30 fee
  • Wire Transfer: International, $25 fee, 5-7 days
  • Check by Mail: $5 fee, 10-14 business days
  • Direct Deposit: US only, 2-3 business days

Refund Policy & Process

Learn about our refund policy, how to request refunds, processing times, and conditions for eligibility.

Refund Policy:
  • Full Refund Period: 30 days from purchase date
  • Partial Refunds: After 30 days, case-by-case basis
  • Processing Time: 7-10 business days for approval
  • Refund Method: Original payment method only
  • Non-refundable: Digital goods after download
Request Process:
  1. Go to Purchase History in your account
  2. Select the item you wish to refund
  3. Choose reason from dropdown menu
  4. Submit additional details if required
  5. Receive confirmation email with reference number
  6. Check refund status in your dashboard

Billing Troubleshooting

Resolve common billing issues: declined payments, failed transactions, billing disputes, and account holds.

Common Issues & Solutions:
  1. Declined Payment:
    • Check card expiration date
    • Verify sufficient funds
    • Contact bank to remove block
    • Try alternative payment method
  2. Double Charge:
    • Wait 24 hours for pending charge to clear
    • Check bank statement for actual postings
    • Contact support with transaction IDs
  3. Currency Conversion Issues:
    • Check exchange rate at time of transaction
    • Verify your bank's conversion fees
    • Use PayPal for better currency handling
  4. Subscription Not Canceling:
    • Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal
    • Check cancellation confirmation email
    • Contact support for immediate termination
